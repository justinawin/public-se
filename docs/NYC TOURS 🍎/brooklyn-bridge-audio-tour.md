## Steeped in History and Free to Visit!

The Brooklyn Bridge, completed in 1883, is one of New York City’s most iconic landmarks and a stunning feat of engineering. Spanning the East River, it connects Manhattan and Brooklyn, offering incredible panoramic views of both boroughs, as well as the Statue of Liberty and the New York skyline.

Walking or biking across the bridge is a memorable experience because of its scenic views and historic charm. The bridge’s pedestrian path, elevated above the roadway, gives you an up-close look at its intricate cables and towering Gothic-style arches. Walking allows you to take in the sights at your own pace, snap photos, and absorb the energy of the city. Biking, on the other hand, is a thrilling way to cross the bridge faster, with a bit of a breeze and the excitement of riding alongside New Yorkers and tourists alike.
